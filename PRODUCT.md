# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners and small business owners in Karachi who need a licensed electrician: wiring, panel upgrades, lighting, EV charger/generator install, inspections, or an emergency repair. Many arrive stressed (an outage, a safety issue, a landlord deadline) and on a phone. The job to be done is fast: confirm this electrician is licensed and legitimate, then call or WhatsApp immediately. Contact happens by phone/WhatsApp, not a contact form, which is the local norm.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing site (Bijlifix) whose only job is converting a visitor into a phone call or WhatsApp message. Success = tap-to-call / tap-to-WhatsApp clicks, fast mobile load, and looking credible enough in the first five seconds that a stressed visitor doesn't bounce to search again.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, prompt, no-nonsense. Feels like a professional who shows up on time and knows the code, not a flashy tech startup and not an unlicensed side-hustle. Emotional goal: reassurance first ("this is a real, licensed electrician"), readiness second ("they can come now").

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-template SaaS landing page: centered hero over a gradient blob, three identical feature cards, tiny uppercase eyebrows over every section.
- Fly-by-night contractor sites: stock-photo-only hero with no real specifics, vague "we serve your area," fake urgency countdowns, wall-of-text pricing.

## Design Principles

1. Trust before flash - licensing, experience, and a real phone number visible without scrolling or digging.
2. Mobile-first urgency - Call and WhatsApp are reachable in one thumb-tap from anywhere on the page.
3. Local specificity - name actual Karachi areas served, not generic "your neighborhood" copy.
4. Calm confidence, not sales pressure - no countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no shouting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum contrast throughout. Touch targets ≥44px (mobile-heavy, often rushed audience). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Dual light/dark support following system preference.
